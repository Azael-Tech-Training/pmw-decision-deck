# Learning Notes & Preferences

## User Profile
- **Current Level**: Intermediate in general electronics. Understands voltage, current, Ohm's law, and transistors. Needs to bridge this theoretical electronics knowledge to practical automotive applications.
- **Interests**: Complete lifecycle of learning PWM: historical context, physics/signal theory, vehicle diagnosis, ECU operation, and custom hobbyist microcontroller design.

## Pedagogical Strategy
- Leverage existing understanding of transistors to explain PWM switching (MOSFETs/BJTs as switches).
- Provide immediate diagnostic ties (what does a bad PWM signal feel/look like on a car?).
- Design interactive, visually stunning HTML-based lessons with embedded widgets (simulators, interactive scopes).
- Focus on retrieval practice and progressive complexity.
