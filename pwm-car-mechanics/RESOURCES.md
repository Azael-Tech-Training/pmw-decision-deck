# Automotive PWM Resources

## Knowledge

- [Guides: PicoScope Automotive Guided Tests - Actuators](https://www.picoauto.com/library/guided-tests)
  Step-by-step guides for hookups and diagnostic parameters for specific PWM actuators (solenoids, fans, fuel pumps). Use for: waveform references and instrument setup parameters.
- [Book: _Engine Performance Diagnostics_ — Paul Danner (ScannerDanner)](https://www.scannerdanner.com/)
  Highly regarded text on automotive electric diagnostics. Use for: understanding oscilloscope hookups, waveform analysis, and testing solid-state drivers.
- [Book: _Automotive Technology: Principles, Diagnosis, and Service_ — James D. Halderman](https://www.jameshalderman.com/)
  Industry-standard automotive textbook. Use for: high-level explanations of how Engine Control Units (ECUs) utilize PWM for actuator speed and position control.
- [Videos: ScannerDanner - How to Find a Short to Ground](https://www.youtube.com/user/ScannerDanner)
  In-depth diagnostic videos demonstrating how to trace shorts using test lights, voltage drops, and Ohm's law without constantly blowing fuses.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [Reddit: r/diyelectronics](https://www.reddit.com/r/diyelectronics/)
  A active community of electronics hobbyists. Use for: getting circuit reviews and coding help when designing custom PWM fan controllers.
- [Reddit: r/mechanicadvice](https://www.reddit.com/r/mechanicadvice/)
  A community of professional and DIY mechanics. Use for: practical car repair advice, identifying standard wire colors, and finding component locations.
- [Forums: Speeduino Engine Management](https://speeduino.com/forum/)
  The community behind the open-source Speeduino ECU. Use for: understanding how automotive microcontrollers handle PWM scheduling, maps, and driver circuits in real-world DIY builds.
